Un petit peu... about me

It has been more than 15 years in which my passion and good spirit has been in the animation industry.
From art direction to visual development, I shares my colored artistic eye and crazy ideas in order to find the best solutions 
in the interest of the project. 

Also I had the opportunity to co-found a independant video game studio “Brain Up” with my friend Vincent E. Sousa in 2021. 
We created three prototypes using Unreal, for which I was primarily responsible for art direction and level design.
These projects were well received and received support from the CNC three times.

After four years of investment, I finally decided to go back to being a freelance solo artist, and ready to take on new creative challenges.

I'm currently in France, but I am flexible depending on opportunities.
