Muffin Jack

2013

Studio :  Ankama Animation

Position : 

Author & Visual development 


Authors : Vincent E Sousa, Denis Bouyer, Marc Bouyer, Yann De Preval, Max Loubaresse, Anthony Vivien

Pitch :


Muffin Jack and his friend Jean-François, a bakery store employee, kicking ass as they face off with evil creatures in town. 

This project was the very first one we worked on right after finishing our studies. Anthony Roux “ToT” from Ankama gave us the opportunity to create a project in the same spirit as our student films Meet Buck and Salesman Pete. It was completely different from what Ankama usually produces. We created a teaser in 2D/3D , which you can see at the bottom of the page, for Annecy and used it to pitch the animated series to broadcasters.
