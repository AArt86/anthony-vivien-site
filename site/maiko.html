Maiko

2024
studio : Brain Up
Position : 

Co-Author, Art Director & Level Designer


Time of production : 1 year and a half Elevator Pitch :


Maiko is a top down action-adventure game. A mix between Death’s Door and Tunic with some Ori’s touch in a nippon themed action-adventure. Katanas, cults and madness are what drives this quest !

For nearly two years, I worked with Vincent ( more involved in game design, 3D supervision, and combat feel) to develop a prototype with financial support from the CNC. Maxime was the first developer to join the team, followed by Danny. And Robin Alonzo, internship on the project. Thank you all team for your hard work.

My work :


I was responsible for creating visual designs related to our lore, as well as providing creative input on the characters designed 
by Alexis Rives.  I worked on designing the prototype level in Miro to establish the tutorial at the beginning of the game, as well as the progression of discovering various items to ensure smooth player progression. Then, I used the tools available to me from the developers to create the level in Unreal.
